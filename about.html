About Us

A licensed electrical contractor and interior partner that takes full responsibility for your space, from concept to completion.

Who We Are

Powering spaces, building trust

R.B Electricals & Enterprises is a Government Certified Class - 1 Licensed Electrical Contractor based in Ropena Agrahara, Bengaluru.

We provide comprehensive solutions in interiors, electrical and turnkey project execution for commercial, corporate and residential spaces. Our clients range from fast-growing technology companies to managed office providers and design studios.

With a skilled team, quality materials and a firm commitment to timelines, we transform spaces into functional, aesthetic and efficient environments, and we stay accountable for every part of the job.
